## Guild Info
Firstly it is recommended to read through the guild vision section so you'll know if this guild is something you'd like to be a part of.
When you decide you'd like to join us, please check out the getting started section

**Gerrit’s Vision for Our Guild**\
**[NL.] Goud Graaiers:**\
To foster a community where diverse playstyles and languages converge for seamless and enjoyable cooperation.

**Faction Alignment and Your Gameplay:**\
Our guild operates as a flotilla and will continue to do so unless a WIC / Dutch faction is introduced to the game, or when we have build up our guild cohesion and strength for port battles, at which point we can pledge our allegience if the majority of our members wishes for a pledge.\
So what does this mean for you, our valued members?

*   **No Faction Affiliation:**
We are not tied to any specific faction, meaning we cannot participate in port battles.

*   **Freedom to Engage:**
There are no reputation requirements within our guild. Members are free to engage with other factions as they see fit. (Please note: We strive to maintain friendly relations with other Dutch guilds.)

*   **Neutrality for Safety:**
By maintaining an allied reputation with all factions, you significantly reduce the amount of hostile encounters from other players.

**Our Unique Approach: How We Operate**

**Organized Squads:**\
Experience coordinated team play with well-crafted strategies designed to ensure collective safety and maximize profit for everyone. Each member plays a crucial role, eliminating the risk of anyone being left vulnerable.

Every organized squad features a dedicated squad leader in voice chat to provide clear directions. Squad leaders can also communicate with each other across different voice channels, facilitating multi-squad cooperation. For optimal teamwork, we highly recommend joining your squad leader’s voice channel on TeamSpeak.

While you are welcome to casually join an organised squad without voice chat, please be aware that TeamSpeak is our primary communication method for coordinated efforts, with in-game chat being secondary. Squad leaders find it challenging to manage situations effectively while constantly typing. Therefore, if you choose to tag along without voice communication, effective communication with your squad will be limited, and you will need to independently monitor your squad mates’ movements.

**Chill / Laid-Back Squads:**\
We understand that not every session calls for intense focus. Our chill squads are perfect for those times when you want to relax, socialize, and sail together without the pressure of leading or strictly following a squad leader.

Chill squads can opt for voice chat or remain entirely text-based. However, it is advisable for the squad leader to be in a TeamSpeak squad channel to facilitate communication with other squad leaders, should the need arise.

## Joining the guild
Before joining our guild, we highly recommend you read the info section above to get to know us better!

#### Joining ingame
To join our guild in the game follow these steps:
*   In the top right corner of your screen click icon for "Guild Ranking"
*   Select the "Guild Ranking" tab again
*   Search for "NL." and click on **"Goud Graaiers"** in the search results
*   On this menu you can send a join application and one of our members will accept your application as soon as possible.

#### Joining the teamspeak
TeamSpeak is our primary way of communication, while it is not a must and we do not enforce it's use, it is a very useful tool that helps us coordinate in events and battles.

If you already have teamspeak installed on your computer you can connect to it by using the domain: `donkey.beauty`
