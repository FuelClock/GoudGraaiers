Plan your trade loop

The planner uses the per-port market prices from the flotilla trading spreadsheet, combined with the game's own port data: every port's shallow-water tier (a ship of rate 3, for example, cannot dock at a tier 5 port) and whether it sits inside the PvP circle. It chains the best counterclockwise legs into one loop: buy where a good is cheap, sell one leg further on.

Your loop

    Stock: every market is a little different each cycle

    Port markets restock on a server cycle (roughly every 24 to 48 hours, depending on your server), and which goods a port stocks after a cycle is partly random: one visit a port may only have Wine, the next the whole range. Guild production and how much players buy shift the amounts further, so treat the route as the plan and the market as the weather. Two practical consequences are built into the planner: every leg lists backup goods in case the main pick is sold out, and remember that provision goods (Beer, Nuts, Dates) are almost always in stock but pay little per cargo weight, so they are hold filler, not the main cargo.

    How the route is chosen

    Every leg in the loop buys the single most profitable good at its start port and sells it at a port further along the counterclockwise direction, ranked by profit per unit of cargo weight. The next leg then starts where the last one ended, until the loop closes. Ports you cannot dock at are left out before any of that happens, so the loop always respects your ship and your tolerance for PvP.

    Data sources: market prices from the flotilla trading spreadsheet, shallow-water tiers and port types straight from the game's own world files, and the PvP circle as marked on community charts. If a port's shallow tier changes with a game patch, the data table is one file and easy to update.
