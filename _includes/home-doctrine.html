Gerrit’s vision

Gerrit’s vision for our guild: to foster a community where diverse playstyles and languages converge for seamless and enjoyable cooperation.

Faction alignment: we operate as a flotilla in game and intend to keep it that way, unless the game introduces a Dutch (WIC) faction, or the guild has built the cohesion and strength for port battles and a majority of our members wishes for a pledge.

What that means for you:

  • No faction affiliation: we are not tied to any specific faction, so we do not take part in port battles.
  • Freedom to engage: no reputation requirements. Members engage other factions as they see fit; we only keep friendly relations with the other Dutch guilds.
  • Neutrality for safety: allied standing with every faction significantly reduces the hostile encounters you meet.

The squads above are how this vision sails day to day.
