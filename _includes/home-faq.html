Before you board

Must I be Dutch?

No. We are a Dutch and English flotilla: about two thirds of us are Dutch, and everything here is written in English. The flag is flown for everyone aboard.

Do I need TeamSpeak?

It is not enforced, but it is how organized squads coordinate: donkey.beauty. Squad leaders give calls by voice; listening in is already a big help.

Is the guild in a faction?

No. We sail as a neutral flotilla with allied standing across the factions, which keeps hostile encounters rare. We stay friendly with the Dutch guilds. If the game adds a Dutch line, or the majority of the fleet wants a pledge, we may pledge together.

What will we actually do?

Everything the sea offers: war hunts, boarding raids, trade loops, expeditions and quiet cruises, in organized squads or chill squads.

How do ranks work?

Cabin Boy is where you start. Sail with us on TeamSpeak weekly and Midshipman shows you every member on the map. Admirals host the voyages and run the fleet. The full ladder is on The Crew.

What if I sail casually?

Fine by us. Freedom to engage is the rule: no reputation requirements, no forced voice. Read Gerrit’s vision above and see whether it fits you.
