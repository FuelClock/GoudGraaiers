Finding the fleet

Open the Guild Ranking panel in-game, search for the tag NL., and send your application to Goud Graaiers. An admiral will accept you aboard, usually the same evening.

How to board, step by step
Search

Guild Ranking tab, top right of the screen in-game.

Apply

Pick Goud Graaiers under the NL. tag and send your application.

Set sail

An admiral accepts you aboard and you’re in the fleet.
