De Goud Graaiers is a Dutch and English flotilla, about two thirds of us Dutch, all proud to sail under the flag. As a neutral flotilla we keep allied standing with every faction, which keeps our seas calm and our options open. War hunts, boarding raids, trade loops, quiet cruises. Gerrit’s vision is a fleet where different playstyles and languages converge into one crew that simply enjoys sailing together.
