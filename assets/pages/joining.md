## Joining in-game

To join our guild in the game follow these steps:

*   In the top right corner of your screen click the icon for **Guild Ranking**
*   Select the **Guild Ranking** tab again
*   Search for **NL.** and click on **Goud Graaiers** in the results
*   On that menu you can send a join application, and one of our admirals will accept you as soon as possible

Before joining, it helps to read [how the flotilla sails](/) — organized and chill squads, neutrality, and what the ranks mean. Most of all: be a shipmate. That is the whole test.
