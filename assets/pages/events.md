### Slave Capturing
**

**What will we be doing?**
Be sure to gather at Al-Khalif port before the scheduled time as this is the time we will leave the port together.
We'll sail to the northeast of the port and we'll be blockading the 2 trade routes to capture all the ships we can and have their crew "voluntarily change employer".
Depending on how many people show up we might need dedicated hauling as the ammount of merchants is considerable.
We'll work together to capture as many slaves as possible so be sure to bring a boarding loadout. If you don't want to or dont have a boarding loadout to bring, you're more then welcome to help us get the health of the enemy ships down so your fellow squad mates can do the boarding instead as we will be sharing the loot in the end anyway.

### Escudo Hunt on the Endless Sea
**

**What will we be doing?**
We will be grouping up at Freedom Bay, at the scheduled time we will have a short briefing after which we'll sail east into the Endless Sea.
On the Endless Sea we'll travel down to the Altar to hunt around that area.
The goal is efficiency and fun!
**Useful Info**
We'll spend quite some time on the endless sea, be sure to bring alot of cannonballs/wood/food etc.
Endless Sea is a PVP zone, you might want to consider this when deciding which crew/ship to bring.
